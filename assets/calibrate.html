Zero calibration

ControlCalibrationServo setupController

After the robot is wired, start calibration. Torque stays off until you move a joint's slider; that joint then powers up at KP = 3, KD = 0 and moves slowly to the slider.

Procedure

Move all to zero only after calibrating. It powers every responding joint, one at a time, and moves each slowly to its saved zero, then holds it there at KP = 3 so you can fine-tune with the sliders and press Finish again. A joint that has not been calibrated moves to an old or factory zero and may bind. Support the robot first.

Policy and pose control stop while this page is active. Each slider follows its joint's encoder until you drag it. Dragging powers that one joint and moves it at about 35°/s (raw encoder 0–4095). Place the joint at its mechanical zero, then press Finish. Finish shifts that servo's stored position offset so this pose reads 2048 and saves 2048 as its zero, leaving about ±180° before the encoder wraps. A joint reading outside 0–4095 can still be finished. A green row means Finish was pressed during this session. Leaving or restarting clears that highlight. It is not written into the saved zero by itself.
