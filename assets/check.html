How to use

Torque must be off: press Torque off first. For each row, hold the robot, press Start, then move only that joint (or the whole robot, for IMU rows) by about 20–30° in the direction described. The row turns green if the robot measures the motion with the sign the walk policy was trained with, and red if the sign is reversed. Directions come from the Microduck model the policies use (robot frame: x forward, y left, z up). "Left" and "right" are the robot's own.

A reversed joint means its servo is mounted the other way round. Press Flip direction on that row, then run the row again to confirm it turns green. Flips are saved on the robot in data/joint_signs.json and applied at every start; calibration zeros are unaffected. A reversed IMU row can't be fixed here; check how the board is mounted.
