Head and mouth

Head 1–4 are neck pitch, head pitch, head yaw and head roll (IDs 30–33), each ±1. In Default pose they move those joints directly, by up to ±30°, ±35°, ±60° and ±20°. While walking they are inputs to the walk policy, which decides how far the head follows. The mouth is motor 34, 0–30°, and is sent with the 50 ms command heartbeat. It is not part of the policy observation.

Home lean · walking backward
0°

Stands the hips and ankles this many degrees more upright than the home pose (Default pose and Walk), moving the body forward over the feet by about 1.3 mm per degree. Use it when the robot's centre of mass is behind the trained one, for example when it tips backward. The applied lean blends from the standing/forward value to the backward value as the walk command goes from 0 to −0.2 backward, changing at most 20°/s. Change these while not walking.

Command smoothing
off

Low-pass on walk and head commands (time constant). Sticks and keys then reach the policy as ramps instead of steps. Try 0.1–0.2 s.

IMU level
—

With torque off, hold the robot so its body is level: put a spirit level (or a phone level app) on the body's flat top and adjust until it reads level both front-to-back and side-to-side. Keep it still and press Level IMU. It averages the accelerometer for 1 s and stores the roll/pitch that makes a level body read as level. Don't use Default pose for this: the head's weight and servo sag can tilt the body a few degrees, and levelling would hide that real lean from the policy.

Servo stiffness · KP
6

Proportional gain of every joint servo in Default pose and Walk (default 6). Higher follows the policy more closely; too high buzzes, oscillates or runs hot. Raise in steps of 2.

Servo damping · KD
20

Derivative gain (default 20). Lower responds faster but can overshoot; upstream Microduck runs with none.

Last walk (flight recorder)
—

Follow ratio = how much of each commanded leg swing the servos achieved (1.0 = all of it), with the delay. It covers all walking in the recorder's last 20 s and updates every 2 s. Press Clear before each gain change so the numbers only cover the new setting.
